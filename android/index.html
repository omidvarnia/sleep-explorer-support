Sleep Explorer

A privacy-first app for Android phones, for adults (18+) to explore how their own nights and days go together. A self-observation tool: not a research study and not a medical device.

Support

Questions, problems or suggestions? Email amir.omidvarnia@gmail.com. Please mention your phone model, its Android version and the app version (shown in More ⋯ → Settings → About).

Common questions

  • Why are my nights missing? Sleep Explorer reads sleep that your watch or ring app writes to Health Connect; check that app syncs to Health Connect and that Sleep Explorer has permission in Health Connect → App permissions.
  • Are the sleep stages exact? No. Core (light), Deep and REM stages are estimated by your watch or ring and are not the same as a sleep-laboratory recording.
  • When will I see patterns? After about two weeks, once the app has learned your usual sleep and has enough paired days.
  • How do I delete my data? In the app, open More ⋯ → My data and choose “Delete all my data…”, or simply delete the app.
  • How do I get updates? Updates come through Google Play. You can also check for an update in Settings → About.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026. Sleep Explorer is developed by Amir Omidvarnia (amir.omidvarnia@gmail.com). It does not collect any data: there is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no network connection. The developer never receives your data.

  • Health data (sleep, and overnight heart rate, heart-rate variability, respiratory rate, wrist temperature and blood oxygen) are read from Health Connect only with your permission. The app only reads this data and never writes to Health Connect. Health data are never stored in a cloud backup. It uses it only to show your own sleep and patterns on your phone.
  • Your answers, task results and the Health data the app reads are stored encrypted on your phone and never leave it, unless you choose to export them yourself.
  • Nothing is shared with the developer or any third party, and nothing is used for advertising.
  • You can pause or stop recording, export a copy of your data, or delete everything at any time in More ⋯ → My data, or by deleting the app.
  • Sleep Explorer is meant for adults (18 or older).
  • Links in the app (support, this policy, the Google Play page, reference DOIs) open in your web browser or Google Play; the app itself sends nothing.

Not a medical device

Sleep Explorer is a self-observation tool, not a research study. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. If you are worried about your sleep or health, please talk to a doctor.
