Sleep Explorer

A privacy-first iPhone and Apple Watch app for adults (18+) to explore how their own nights and days go together. A self-observation tool: not a research study and not a medical device.

Support

Questions, problems or suggestions? Email amir.omidvarnia@gmail.com. Please mention your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version (shown in More ⋯ → Settings → About).

Common questions

  • Why are my nights missing? Sleep Explorer reads sleep recorded by Apple Watch in the Health app. Check that Sleep Tracking is on in the Watch app and that Sleep Explorer has permission under Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.
  • Are the sleep stages exact? No. Core, Deep and REM stages are estimated by Apple and are not the same as a sleep-laboratory recording.
  • When will I see patterns? After about two weeks, once the app has learned your usual sleep and has enough paired days.
  • How do I delete my data? In the app, open More ⋯ → My data and choose “Delete all my data…”, or simply delete the app.
  • How do I get updates? Updates come through the App Store. Turn on App Updates in iPhone Settings → App Store, or tap “Check for updates in the App Store” in Settings → About.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026. Sleep Explorer is developed by Amir Omidvarnia (amir.omidvarnia@gmail.com). It does not collect any data: there is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no network connection. The developer never receives your data.

  • Health data (sleep, and overnight heart rate, heart-rate variability, respiratory rate, wrist temperature and blood oxygen) are read from Apple Health only with your permission. The app never writes to Health.
  • Your answers, task results and the Health data the app reads are stored encrypted on your iPhone and never leave it, unless you choose to export them yourself.
  • Nothing is shared with the developer or any third party, and nothing is used for advertising.
  • You can pause or stop recording, export a copy of your data, or delete everything at any time in More ⋯ → My data, or by deleting the app.
  • Sleep Explorer is meant for adults (18 or older).
  • Links in the app (support, this policy, the App Store page, reference DOIs) open in Safari or the App Store; the app itself sends nothing.

Not a medical device

Sleep Explorer is a research and self-observation tool. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. If you are worried about your sleep or health, please talk to a doctor.
